Get started with TapeMicro

TapeMicro splits TapeOut and Behemoth miners into Micro shares of 1H each. Buy from 1H, trade on the order book, claim $BEM based on your shares, and redeem the original miner when you own all its shares.

01 / ABOUT TAPEMICRO

One original miner = N shares of 1H

Each miner is held in its own custody contract. Shares are issued 1:1 against its deposited hashrate, and share that miner's actual BEM output. Rewards depend on the upstream mining protocol and network hashrate.

02 / PREPARATION

Prepare a BSC wallet and some BNB

TapeMicro runs on BNB Smart Chain. You confirm every operation in your own wallet. The website never needs your private key or seed phrase.

  1. Install a wallet

    On desktop, use a browser wallet extension with an EVM connection interface. On mobile, open tapemicro.top in your wallet app's built-in browser. WalletConnect QR connections are not currently supported.

  2. Switch to BSC mainnet

    Select BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56). The website prompts you to switch if you are on a different network.

  3. Prepare BNB

    An instant purchase costs the trade value plus a 1% fee. Every on-chain operation also needs BNB for gas; reserve enough according to your wallet's estimate.

  4. Click “Connect wallet” at the top right

    Connecting lets the site read your address and on-chain data. Itdoes not approve asset transfers and does not move your assets.

Use the official domain: tapemicro.top. Do not connect to lookalike links or “airdrop claim” pages sent in chats or direct messages.
03 / SPLITTING

Split an original miner into Micro shares

For TapeOut / Behemoth owners who want to sell part of a miner or make it easier to trade. Splitting has no protocol fee. You initially receive all the shares and keep their rewards.

  1. Select the original miner

    After connecting, the site loads miners in your wallet. You can also look up a miner by collection and number. Ineligible miners show a reason.

  2. Check the eligible H

    The number of shares equals the miner's verified hashrate. BEM accrued before the split is paid to your wallet during the deposit.

  3. Two wallet confirmations

    First approvethis one NFT (approve, for this token ID only), then confirm the split. The Micro shares arrive directly in your wallet.

Which miners are eligible?

The contract checks eligibility at deposit time; the frontend cannot bypass it:

Accepted
  • TapeOut or Behemoth miners
  • Active, with all hashrate verified
  • Pass the official circuit counterexample check
Not accepted
  • First-invention miners with a design bonus
  • Unverified or partly unverified miners
  • Failed, invalidated or inactive miners
Deposit only through the “Split” button. Sending an NFT directly to the vault or a custody address with transferFrom does not mint Micro shares. There is no recovery function.
04 / BUYING

Buy Micro: buy now or place a bid

Use “Buy now” for an immediate purchase. Use “Place bid” to name your price and wait for someone to sell to you.

Buy now

  1. Enter the H you want to buy

    The site matches the cheapest asks first, including shares of different miners. One transaction supports up to 16 fills.

  2. Check the quote before confirming

    Check the trade value, 1% fee and total payment, then confirm in your wallet.Buying requires no token approval; the wallet should only request the BNB payment.

  3. Receive shares and start earning BEM

    Normally, the seller's old rewards are harvested and settled first, and you share subsequent output from the trade onward. If the official harvest fails, unharvested BEM follows the shares to their new owner.

If the order book changes before execution, such as another buyer filling an ask or a seller cancelling it, the transaction reverts without an extra trade payment. Gas may still be charged. Review a fresh quote.

Place a bid

  1. Choose the accepted miners

    “Any miner” accepts tradable miners already in the pool and those deposited later. Each miner has separate rewards and risks. Choose “Selected miners” if you only want specific ones.

  2. Set your price and amount

    If your bid is at or above an eligible ask, the page suggests “Buy now” for a faster purchase at the available ask price.

  3. Escrow BNB and wait for a seller

    Placing a bid locks amount × price in the market contract. When someone fills it, the shares go directly to your wallet.

  • Placing a bid has no protocol fee. The seller filling it pays the 1% fee; you pay your bid price.
  • Cancel the unfilled portion at any time for a full refund of its escrowed BNB. Expired unfilled bids can also be cancelled for a refund.
  • The contract rejects inactive or abnormal miner shares when filling your bid.
05 / SELLING

Sell Micro: fill a bid or place an ask

Use “Sell now” to fill existing bids immediately. Use “List for sale” to set your own price and wait.

Sell now

Enter the H to sell. The site matches the highest bids that accept your miner's shares. You receive the proceeds after the 1% fee.

List for sale

Select shares of one miner, then set the price per H, amount and expiry (1 / 7 / 30 days). Placing an ask is free; the buyer pays the 1% fee.

How rewards are handled

Normally, old rewards are settled before the transfer. BEM already settled stays with the seller. If the official harvest fails, unharvested BEM follows the shares to the buyer.

  1. Select shares

    Each ask sells shares of one original miner. List different miners separately.

  2. Set the price, amount and expiry

    If your ask is at or below a compatible bid, the page suggests selling directly to that bid for a faster fill.

  3. Approve once before your first listing or sale

    The wallet requests setApprovalForAll, allowing the market contract to transfer your Micro shares (across all miner IDs in the vault). It does not include BNB, BEM or your original miner NFTs.

  4. Listed shares stay in your wallet

    The listing reserves that amount, but shares move only when a buyer fills it. Cancel any unfilled portion at any time.

  5. Receive sale proceeds

    Proceeds are sent directly to your wallet. If your contract wallet rejects BNB, they are recorded as pending proceeds and can be withdrawn to an address you choose.

Cancel old asks after transferring shares away. Transferring shares to another wallet does not automatically release old listing reservations. The page marks those asks as unfillable; cancel them before listing again.
06 / REWARDS

Claim BEM from your Micro shares

Each miner accrues rewards separately, distributed by shares. Settled rewards remain with their owner. If the official harvest fails, unharvested rewards follow transferred shares.

  1. Select miners to claim from

    The page lists your claimable BEM by miner, including settled rewards from miners whose shares you have already sold.

  2. Confirm claim

    The vault harvests the latest output from the official mining contract, then distributes your share. Many miners may require several transactions; you can resume an interrupted claim session.

If the official claim temporarily fails: The page shows that the official harvest is unavailable. Retry later, or use “Claim synced BEM” for rewards already in the vault. Unharvested output can be retried; if you transfer shares or redeem first, it follows the shares or the original NFT.
BEM has 8 decimal places. Small holdings may earn only small fractions of a BEM per day.
07 / REDEMPTION

Own all of a miner's shares to redeem its NFT

Shares let you hold a portion of the original miner's output. Redeem only when you want the whole original NFT back in your wallet.

  1. Collect 100% of its shares

    A 100H miner requires all 100 shares of that same miner. Shares from other miners cannot substitute for them.

  2. Click “Redeem NFT” on its row in Rewards

    The button appears when you own 100% of that miner's shares. Confirm to burn them and receive the original NFT. There is no protocol fee.

  3. Settled BEM remains claimable

    BEM already allocated to you before redemption is unaffected and can still be claimed afterward.

Lost shares can make full redemption impossible. Shares sent to an unrecoverable address can prevent anyone from collecting 100%. Other shares can still mine, claim and trade while the miner remains active. After redemption, old asks cannot fill; cancel them.

11 / FAQ

FAQ

Rewards
Do Micro shares earn the same as the original miner?
Shares receive a proportional share of their original miner's actual output. For example, 10 shares of a miner split into 100 shares receive 10% of that miner's rewards. Network hashrate and upstream rules affect the output.
When do rewards start after buying?
Normally from the trade onward, after the seller's old rewards are settled. If the official harvest fails, unharvested BEM follows the shares to the buyer; settled rewards remain with the seller.
Why is my BEM claim so small?
Daily output varies with network hashrate, emission parameters and miner status. Check the trade page's latest estimate per 100H. Actual rewards are determined by on-chain claims.
Do unclaimed rewards expire?
BEM already settled for you is recorded on-chain and stays claimable after a sale or redemption. If the official harvest fails, unharvested output follows transferred shares or the redeemed original NFT.
What if the original miner stops or becomes abnormal?
Its shares become untradable: they cannot be listed, bought or sold into bids. Accrued rewards remain claimable, and all shares can still be redeemed together for the original NFT.
Trade
Are shares from different miners interchangeable?
No. Each original miner has its own asset ID and reward accounting. Holdings appear in separate rows. A purchase can combine fills across miners, but the received assets remain separate.
Can I cancel asks and bids?
Yes. Cancelling an ask releases its reservation. Cancelling a bid returns the full escrow for its unfilled portion. Expired orders can also be cleared for a refund.
Why did the match cover only part of my amount?
Matching is limited by loaded order-book depth and a maximum of 16 fills per transaction. The quote shows the matched amount before confirmation. Execution is atomic: a stale quote reverts the whole batch rather than skipping failed fills. Review a new quote before submitting again.
Why has the page not updated after my wallet confirmation?
The transaction still needs an on-chain receipt. Open “Transaction history” at the top right for its status and BscScan link. The page refreshes after confirmation; use “Refresh” if it remains unchanged.
Why did sale proceeds not arrive directly?
If your contract wallet cannot receive BNB, proceeds are recorded as pending. Withdraw them from the trade page to an address that accepts BNB.
Splitting & redemption
Can I send the NFT directly to the vault?
No. Use the “Split” button. A direct transfer does not mint Micro shares and has no recovery function.
Why is my miner ineligible for splitting?
Only ordinary verified miners are accepted. First inventions, unverified, failed or invalid miners are ineligible. The contract also runs the official counterexample check during deposit and rejects miners that fail it.
Does splitting deduct rewards or shares?
There is no split fee. A 100H miner mints all 100 shares to you. Rewards accrued before deposit are paid to you during the split.
Contact & support

When reporting an issue, include the page, wallet address and transaction hash. Never provide private keys, seed phrases or verification codes.

Risks

Original miners may stop or become abnormal. BEM prices and network output fluctuate; past output does not predict future rewards. Official harvesting may temporarily fail. Smart contracts may contain unknown vulnerabilities and have not undergone an independent third-party audit. Only commit funds you can afford to lose.
